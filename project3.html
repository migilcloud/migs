Overview

A complete online presence for a photography studio.

Studio8teen is a modern web application built for Studio 8Teen Photography, a professional studio offering sessions, weddings, birthdays, photobooth rentals, and corporate event coverage. The site gives clients a clear way to explore services, browse the portfolio, compare packages, and get in touch — all through a responsive, visually refined interface.

Problem

The studio needed a professional online presence to showcase their work, display pricing packages, and let clients book sessions without relying solely on social media.

Solution

Built a full-featured React website with service pages, a portfolio gallery, tiered pricing packages, user registration and login, and an FAQ chatbot for common inquiries.

Outcome

Studio 8Teen now has a live, polished website at studio8teen.org that presents their brand professionally and helps convert visitors into booked clients.

Built with modern tools.

Features

Key functionality

Services & Portfolio
